# Verian Capital - ISO 27001

# 1. **Project Overview**

This project focuses on the practical application of Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC) principles through the development of an Information Security Risk Assessment for a fictional organisation, Verian Capital.

This project represents my first hands-on experience with Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC). Having never undertaken a practical exercise of this nature before, I approached it as an opportunity to put the theoretical knowledge acquired through my studies into practice, explore GRC methodologies and become familiar with industry tools. The project also represents an opportunity to learn through practical application, understand the challenges involved in information security risk management and develop my skills in this field.

The project uses CISO Assistant as its primary GRC platform to organise assets, document risks, manage security controls and support the overall risk assessment process.

The assessment draws upon recognised cybersecurity frameworks and standards, including ISO/IEC 27001 and ISO/IEC 27002, to establish a structured approach to information security governance and risk management.

This documentation describes the methodology, tools, decisions and implementation steps undertaken throughout the project.

# 2. **Company Profile & Scenario**

[Verian Capital](Verian%20Capital%20-%20Profile.pdf) is a mid-sized European financial services firm (about 250 employees) offering wealth management, corporate finance and sustainable investment services, and is preparing for ISO/IEC 27001 certification.

# 3. **Scope & Objectives**

The objective is to create asset, vulnerabilities and risk scenarios lists, and check if the company os ready or not to implement and get its certification on ISO 27001.

### 12.1 Project Results

Work completed:

- Created an asset inventory based on Verian Capital's organisational context.

- Identified and documented eight vulnerabilities.

- Developed eight risk scenarios using an asset-to-risk evaluation approach.

- Applied an ISO 27005 risk matrix to classify risks.

- Documented selected risk treatment controls.

- Conducted an ISO 27001 audit, reviewing clauses and controls individually through CISO Assistant.

### 12.2 Assessment Findings

The assessment provided an initial overview of Verian Capital's information security posture and highlighted areas requiring further attention.

The risk assessment helped establish relationships between information assets, vulnerabilities and potential security risks. The ISO 27001 audit also provided an opportunity to examine the organisation's compliance against individual requirements and controls.

The organization only got 21.95% (27 of compliance of the 123 controls and clauses) of ISO 27001.

### 12.3 Lessons Learned

As my first practical GRC exercise, this project provided valuable insights into how information security risk management works in practice.

Some of the main lessons learned were:

- Understanding the importance of asset identification and classification as the foundation of risk assessment.

- Learning how to translate vulnerabilities into meaningful risk scenarios.

- Understanding the relationship between risk assessment, risk treatment and ISO 27001 controls.

- Gaining practical familiarity with CISO Assistant and its GRC functionalities.

- Recognising the importance of organisational context when evaluating information security risks. 

## 12.4 Final Thoughts

This project was an important first step in applying my theoretical knowledge of Governance, Risk and Compliance to a practical scenario.

Although the assessment was limited in scope and does not represent a complete ISO 27001 implementation or certification readiness evaluation, it allowed me to explore the risk management lifecycle, work with an industry GRC platform and better understand the responsibilities involved in information security governance.

It also provided a foundation for future projects and further development of my knowledge of ISO 27001, ISO 27005 and information security risk management.
